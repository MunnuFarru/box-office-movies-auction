📜 Game Rules

  1. Each player starts with the budget chosen at setup.
  2. A random movie appears in each round. Its sample collection is the score awarded to the owner.
  3. Players bid in turn, raising the price by ₹5 crore each time.
  4. Never bid more than your remaining budget. Passing removes you from that round's auction.
  5. The highest bidder wins the movie and pays their winning bid from their budget.
  6. If the timer runs out, the current highest bidder wins. If nobody bids, the movie is skipped.
  7. After 10 rounds, the player with the highest combined movie collection wins. Ties are possible.

Collections are illustrative sample values, not official or live box-office data.
